Before you start Step 1 of 2 · Preparation

What we are doing

We take a figure from a research paper and turn it into a short, narrated animation that walks through it one piece at a time. You will rate how well those animations do their job.

AnimateBanana

The screen you will use

1 The original figure. Click it to zoom.
2 The animation style it is meant to follow. Open "What this style requires" for the exact rule.
3 Drag the slider, or use ← →, to step through the animation. Reach the end to unlock the questions.
4 Answer in order. A later question only appears when an earlier answer makes it relevant.
5 Submit and continue moves to the next sample. You can change answers until then.
  • Reach the end of the animation before answering; the questions stay hidden until you do.
  • There are no right answers and you do not need to know the paper. Judge what you see.
  • In the comparison part you pick one of two; the other is replaced by a third and you pick again.

A quick check

Answer these the way you would in the real study. It is a check that the task is clear, not a test of the subject matter.
